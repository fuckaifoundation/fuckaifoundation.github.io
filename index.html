Fuck AI !

Why ?

This website started as a knee-jerk reaction to the suffocating invasion of so-called "artificial intelligence" (AI) in our societies, in particular generative AI (GenAI) and Large Language Models (LLMs). We see it as an existential threat and urge governements, organizations, and individuals to withdraw from its development and use.

  • LLMs are being trained on copyrighted material, raising questions as to both the legality and ethics of doing so.
  • LLMs' training and inference uses substantial electricity. In some countries, these extra electricity requirements are met using fossil fuels. These in turn increase greenhouse gas emissions, worsening global warming. In other countries where carbon-neutral and renewable sources of electricity are used, this represents a conflict of use with the decarbonization of other elements of the economy.
  • LLMs represent a threat to how people learn, children and young adults in particular.
  • LLMs are mostly controlled by hyperscaler, hypercapitalistic companies, increasing the risk of oligarchic control over the technologie in question.
  • LLMs participate towards a seemingly never-ending societal trend of "going faster" instead of pushing us to do better. Going faster often translates into a higher consumption rate of finite natural resources.
  • LLMs are destroying jobs and turning some workers into meat proxies.
  • LLMs are destroying the Internet as we know it. Nowadays "prove you are human" challenges are everywhere to limit the effects of webscrappers. More than half of Internet traffic is now due to bots.
  • LLMs contribute to isolation and destroying communities by limiting direct interaction.
